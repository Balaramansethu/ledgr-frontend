## What is Ledgr?

Ledgr is a private, personal money tracker that treats **shared expenses as a
first‑class citizen** — not an afterthought. It does two jobs most people juggle
across two separate apps:

1. **Track your own spending** — budgets, categories, trends.
2. **Track who owes you** when you split a bill.

…and then it does the thing neither side does well: it **nets them together**, so
every number you see reflects *your* real share.

## Why Ledgr stands out

> Most trackers quietly lie to you the moment you split a bill.

You pay **₹4,000** for dinner and your friends owe you **₹3,000** of it. A typical
app logs the whole **₹4,000** against your budget — inflating your spending with
money that's coming right back to you. Ledgr logs your **actual ₹1,000** in every
total, chart, budget, and insight, while tracking the ₹3,000 in your friends'
per‑person ledgers until they settle up.

That split‑aware accounting is the core idea. Around it:

| | Why it's different |
|---|---|
| 🧮 **Split‑aware budgeting** | Budgets, charts, and daily totals count your *real share*, net of what others owe — no phantom spending. |
| 👥 **Two apps in one** | Expense tracking **and** who‑owes‑whom, unified. Per‑person ledgers, running balances, one‑tap settle‑up. |
| 🔮 **Insights that act, not just charts** | "At this pace you'll exceed your budget by ₹X with 9 days left." Week‑over‑week shifts, category spikes, your biggest spending day, a spending "mood." |
| 🔁 **Set‑and‑forget bills** | Rent, subscriptions, and EMIs auto‑log on their day each month — reliably, without ever double‑counting. |
| 🔐 **Your data, actually yours** | One‑tap CSV export, full JSON backup, and restore. No ads, no selling your transactions, no lock‑in. |
| 🌍 **Built for the world** | 15 currencies with locale‑aware compact notation (₹1.5L, ₹2Cr, $1.5k). |
| 📜 **Feels like a ledger, not a bank app** | A deliberate editorial look — warm paper canvas, terracotta accent, serif hero numbers — instead of the interchangeable neon‑gradient fintech template. |

## What you can do

- **Home** — a budget donut, per‑category breakdown, and your recent activity at a glance
- **Add in seconds** — a fast numeric keypad, an optional note, and a category
- **Snap the receipt** — attach a photo to any expense and open it full‑screen in a clean viewer
- **Split anything** — divide a bill across people, watch balances build up, and settle up in a tap
- **Set a budget** — with a calendar heatmap that shows exactly which days you spend
- **Automate the boring bills** — recurring rules that log themselves each month
- **Understand your money** — projections, spending mood, and smart tips, not just graphs
- **Make it yours** — custom categories with your own icons and colors, light or dark
- **Everywhere** — installable as an app, works offline for viewing, and nudges you with a daily reminder
